About Our School

Dedicated to Nurturing Potential & Character

Shree Chautare Danda Secondary School stands as a beacon of learning in Baglung, Nepal. Guided by our foundational belief in "Education for all," we cultivate a welcoming, high-expectation environment.

We strive to combine strong core academics with practical life skills, cultural appreciation, and moral integrity. Our dedicated educators work closely with families and community leaders to ensure every student receives the guidance needed to thrive.

Our Mission

Provide holistic, affordable, and accessible education that prepares learners for lifelong achievement.

Our Vision

A vibrant learning community where equal educational opportunities foster innovation and social responsibility.
