Our Identity

About Shree Chautare Danda

Rooted in Baglung District, dedicated to providing accessible, top-tier secondary education to the community of Bhakunde.

History & Heritage

Established in 2038 B.S. by visionary local leaders and educators, Shree Chautare Danda Secondary School began as a modest community learning center in Ward No. 10 Bhakunde. Over four decades, through public support and government partnership, it has transformed into a comprehensive secondary educational institution serving hundreds of young minds annually across Baglung.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a beacon of academic excellence, innovation, and ethical leadership in Gandaki Province.
Mission: To foster an inclusive learning environment that combines academic fundamentals with digital technology, sports, and civic responsibility, empowering students to succeed locally and globally.

Educational Standards

Curriculum & Extracurriculars

Aligned with Nepal's National Curriculum Framework while fostering sports, technology, and leadership skills.

Primary Level (K-5)

Activity-based learning focusing on literacy, numeracy, social values, and creative play in a safe environment.

Basic Level (6-8)

Integrated science, mathematics, social studies, English, and Nepali, introducing practical lab work and computer education.

Secondary Level (9-12)

Specialized SEE preparation and Higher Secondary streams (Management, Humanities, Science foundational tracks).
